# AirShare

One keyboard and mouse for all your computers. Move your pointer off the edge of one screen and it carries on
onto the next. Your keyboard, clipboard, images and files follow.

Free forever for up to 3 computers. Windows and Linux, side by side.

- **Windows:** [Get it from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NK09P1L0KTJ), or download
  `AirShare-Setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/wallisoft/airshare-releases/releases/latest).
- **Linux** (X11 session): `curl -fsSL https://github.com/wallisoft/airshare-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash`

More at **[airshare.eur.bz](https://airshare.eur.bz)**.
